## Abydonian

Abydonian is a specific type of villager found only in the dimension of Abydos. This villager can acquire any profession, 
but naturally some professions do not appear - most notably the cartographer, fisher and the librarian.

| Armorer | Butcher | Cleric | Farmer | Leatherworker | Nitwit | Shepherd |

Trades:
Abydonian does not have modified trades - they are identical to other villager types.


## Priests
Priest is a specialized villager profession that only spawns in villages with stargate. Therefore, no villager without the profession can become priest.

| Desert | Jungle | Plains | Savanna | Snow | Swamp | Taiga |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ![Desert priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_desert.webp) | ![Jungle priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_jungle.webp) | ![Plains priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_plains.webp) | ![Savanna priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_savanna.webp) | ![Snow priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_snow.webp) | ![Swamp priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_swamp.webp) | ![Taiga priest](/assets/jsg/mobs/priest_taiga.webp) |

Priests perform maintenance on Stargate DHD during their daily routine, and during this maintenance they can dial any 
available 7 glyph address. If stable wormhole is created, priests will pass through the Stargate and a few villagers may follow them.

This is how villagers are exchanged between villages.

Trades:

Each type of priest has unique trade offers. The rarer the type of priest, the rarer its saleable items/blocks are.
