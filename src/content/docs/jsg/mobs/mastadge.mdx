The Mastadge is a large, furry animal found on Abydos. It is well adapted to the desert climate.
Mastadge is a special mob that can only be found in the Abydos dimension near Abydos villages. 
Villagers used them to transport naquadah from the naquadah mines to Abydos pyramid, which they 
sacrificed to their god Ra.

![A Mastadge at a desert camp on Abydos](/assets/jsg/mobs/mastadge.webp)

Now mastadge is only used for traveling through the Abydos Desert or kept as a pet. It can be bred 
using a chocolate bar. If mastadge is equipped with a saddle, it can travel through the desert quite easily.
