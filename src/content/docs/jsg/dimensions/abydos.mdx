Abydos was the first planet visited by a landing party from Earth. At that time, Abydos was still ruled by the Egyptian god Ra. 
Abydos is therefore the first added dimension that can be visited through stargate. Stargate address of Abydos can be obtained 
by founding buried stargate in desert or can be found in the section stargate/reserved addresses.

## Generation

Abydos is a desert planet with 1 sun and 3 moons - which move in different orbits - so they can only be seen at a certain moment 
in the right position behind the pyramid.

Abydos Pyramid is a unique structure - there is only one in the entire dimension, it contains not only stargate, but also the 
treasury of god Ra.

There is also a specific type of village - where you can find mastadge and local residents Abydonians. These villagers who were 
taken here through stargate by the defeated god Ra to mine Naquadah for him in the mines scattered throughout this dimension. 

## Resources

Abydos is a dimension that, in addition to basic materials contains silicon-based material that can be mined in underground 
deposits, in naquadah spires or small naquadah boulders that can be mainly found in this specific dimension and biome.

## Abydos Dungeon

Abydos contains a specific type of dungeon. This dungeon is unique, because it is large underground multi-level maze that contains 
not only treasure rooms, but also rooms with monster spawners. All loot here is completely random, including the content of gold and naquadah.


## Abydos Campfires

Abydos campsites or campfires are structures that generate in open terrain. Generation of structure is random - it may contain campfire, 
empty cauldron, cauldron with water or a bale of hay that serves as food for local fauna. These structures are built by wandering caravans 
and therefore some structures may contain loot. At the same time, a grassy area with a mastadge is also located near this campsite.


## Abydos Cartouche

Abydos Cartouche is structure that contains the address for returning to overworld. This cartouche is very difficult to find - it is an 
old cave whose entrance may have been completely destroyed by weathering over the years and entrance to the cartouche will be just a hole 
in the ground. The cartouche is only structure that appears on map sold by level 5 Abydonian Cartographer. This structure may also contain 
a secret room that was discovered by Daniel Jackson during his year-long stay at Abydos.
